EmailJS Setup Instructions:

1. Sign up for EmailJS at https://www.emailjs.com/
2. Create an Email Service (Gmail recommended)
3. Create an Email Template with these variables:
   - {{from_name}}
   - {{from_email}}
   - {{phone}}
   - {{company}}
   - {{job_title}}
   - {{message}}
   - {{to_email}}
4. Get your Public Key from your EmailJS Account settings
5. Update the .env.local file with your actual credentials:
   NEXT_PUBLIC_EMAILJS_SERVICE_ID=your_service_id
   NEXT_PUBLIC_EMAILJS_TEMPLATE_ID=your_template_id
   NEXT_PUBLIC_EMAILJS_PUBLIC_KEY=your_public_key
6. Restart your development server and test the form
